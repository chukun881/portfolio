Fig. 1 — Final-year project · University Malaysia Sabah · 2025–26

Early detection of Alzheimer’s disease with generative AI

A class-conditional DCGAN — 11.6M-parameter generator, 1,000 epochs on an RTX 2060 — synthesized MRI slices to fight a 50:1 class imbalance. Three transfer-learning classifiers (VGG16, DenseNet201, InceptionV3) were each tuned over ~60 Optuna Bayesian trials, evaluated leakage-safe: synthetic slices in training only.†
